\hladd{Failure-case inspection focuses on the 13 unsuccessful trials in
Case~1 and 29 in Case~2. The interception failures are most plausibly associated
with unfavorable initial geometry followed by sustained target maneuvering,
which produces persistent LOS and time-to-go variations. Under bounded speed
and acceleration, the assigned interceptors may then fail to recover sufficient
closing margin before the attacker reaches the protected region. Delayed
cooperative engagement is different: all attackers are eventually intercepted,
but path asymmetry, maneuver-induced detours, and delayed or noisy state
information cause the time-to-go estimates within one target group to diverge,
leaving a member to arrive late. Thus, the limitations separate into loss of
interception reachability and loss of within-group synchronization, motivating
online reassignment and explicit monitoring of kill feasibility and time-to-go
dispersion.}
