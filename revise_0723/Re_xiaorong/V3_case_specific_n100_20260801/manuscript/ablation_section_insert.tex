\subsection{\hladd{Component Ablation Study}}
\label{subsec:ablation}

\hladd{To identify the independent contribution of the three architectural components, we
construct three one-component-at-a-time controls in addition to complete ART-MAPPO:
``w/o trust-aware mechanism'' disables trust-modulated fusion of the analytical guidance
prior and learned action; ``w/o GRU temporal encoder'' replaces recurrent temporal encoding
with a feedforward representation of the current observation; and ``w/o
attention--residual backbone'' replaces that backbone with a parameter-count-matched
feedforward network while retaining the trust and GRU modules.  All variants use the same
reward, dynamics, optimizer, training seed, and 585-update (599,040-step) training budget.
The selected policies are frozen before testing; each table row contains 1000 Case~1 Monte
Carlo firing tests with no backpropagation or optimizer update.}

\hladd{As summarized in Table~\ref{tab:compact_ablation_v3}, the four training AUCs lie in
the narrow range $535.04$--$535.67\times10^3$, while the final-return standard deviations
span $106.01$--$108.91\times10^3$.  Thus, all variants reach essentially the same nominal
return plateau, and the small single-seed differences in these two aggregate training
statistics should not be interpreted as a universal ranking.  The mechanism-specific logs
nevertheless reveal the intended independent effects: removing trust produces a much larger
entropy contraction (78.5\% versus 52.4\%) and, in the 1000-trial test, increases $E_t$ from
26.830 to 27.248~s, showing that trust regulates exploration and improves closure efficiency
rather than merely increasing AUC.  Removing the GRU raises final Critic loss by $5.31\times$
and increases $E_n$ by 0.00262~g and $E_t$ by 0.107~s, supporting the role of temporal memory
in value estimation and terminal control.  The capacity-matched removal of the
attention--residual backbone increases $E_n$ by 0.00227~g and $E_t$ by 0.032~s, indicating a
smaller but measurable representation and control margin.  All four variants intercept all
targets in 1000/1000 trials; consequently, ISR exhibits a nominal ceiling and the continuous
metrics are required to distinguish the modules.  Complete ART-MAPPO attains the shortest
engagement time, whereas its modest $E_{co\text{-}time}=0.0768$~s remains far below the 0.5-s
cooperation threshold.  These results demonstrate a multi-objective tradeoff rather than an
unsupported claim that the complete model must minimize every individual terminal metric.}
