\noindent\textbf{Reviewer comment:}
\emph{Then, it would be helpful to add an ablation investigation
demonstrating the independent contribution of the trust-aware mechanism, GRU
temporal encoder, and attention-residual backbone within ART-MAPPO
architecture.}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Response:}
Thank you for this constructive suggestion. We added a controlled
component-ablation experiment comparing full ART-MAPPO with three
single-component removals: without trust-aware training, without the GRU
temporal encoder, and without the attention--residual backbone. The MLP used
for the last ablation is parameter matched (actor: 732,774 versus 733,200
parameters; critic: 749,761 versus 749,932), while the environment, reward,
optimizer, target assignment, guidance settings, and rollout budget remain
fixed.

Each variant was trained with five paired seeds in both three-dimensional
interception cases (0.6 and 1.8 million environment steps for Cases 1 and 2).
Every trained seed was evaluated over 20 common held-out deterministic
episodes, yielding 800 episodes. Trust-guided exploration was disabled during
evaluation, so the trust comparison measures its training-time contribution.
We report 95\% Student-\(t\) confidence intervals over seed-level estimates
and use paired two-sided exact sign-flip tests with Holm correction.

Removing the attention--residual backbone gives the clearest degradation:
the full model improves pooled final return by \(1.709\times10^6\), with
\(d_z=0.884\) and \(p_{\mathrm{Holm}}=0.0469\). In Case 2, it also improves
per-target interception and synchronized interception by 6.00 and 6.875
percentage points. The trust-aware mechanism gives directional difficult-case
gains: interception rises from 34.0\% to 44.625\% and synchronization from
24.0\% to 30.5\%, although the pooled corrected target-level tests are not
significant. The full model has a positive pooled final-return difference
over no-GRU (\(d_z=0.678\)), but this comparison is also not significant after
correction and no-GRU remains competitive in Case 2. We therefore revised the
paper to support the backbone contribution strongly, describe the trust
effect as task dependent, and avoid claiming that GRU is universally
indispensable.

For transparency, strict all-target metrics are retained in the data. Full
ART-MAPPO achieves 100\% all-target interception and 90\% all-target
synchronized interception in Case 1. The all-eight-target Case-2 indicator is
zero for every variant at this budget, producing a floor effect; hence the
figure displays per-target rates while the strict metrics remain reported in
the accompanying CSV files. The complete raw curves, 800 episode records,
statistics, code snapshot, and validation records are supplied with the
revision.
